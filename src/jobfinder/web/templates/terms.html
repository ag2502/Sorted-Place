What this site is

Sorted Place is run by Amogh Gaikwad, an individual in Ireland. It lists job adverts read from employers' own careers pages and careers events read from the sites that list them, and links you to the original. It is not an employer, a recruiter or an event organiser, and it is not connected to, or endorsed by, any employer or organiser it lists unless it says so.

Applying and registering happen elsewhere

Apply opens the employer's own posting, and an event's link opens the listing where you register. This site never takes an application, a booking or a payment, and what happens on those sites is between you and them, under their terms.

Listings are as their owners publish them

We read adverts and events automatically and check them often, but we cannot promise any listing is accurate, complete or still open: a role may have been filled, an event moved or cancelled. Always check the original before you rely on it. Facts we read from an advert, such as pay or work mode, are shown only when the advert states them, and are never estimates.

Your account
