Your CV is yours alone.

When you add your CV to your profile it is kept privately in your account: only you can read it, and removing it deletes it outright. We keep the file so that when we improve how CVs are read, yours is read again without you having to upload it.

Who is responsible

Sorted Place is run by Amogh Gaikwad, an individual in Ireland, who decides what is kept and why (the data controller). For anything about your data, or to ask for a listing to be taken down, email {{ contact_email }}. You will get an answer within one month, usually much sooner.

What is kept from your CV

Saved with your account, in the EU, where only you can read it:

  • The file you uploaded, in a private folder of your own
  • What we read from it to rank your searches: skill keywords such as python, the kinds of work it points to, a seniority guess, a rough number of years and a one line summary

Remove on your profile deletes your CV file and everything read from it outright; Replace swaps them for a newer CV. Nothing is archived.

What else an account keeps

An account is optional. Searching and seeing every job work without one; ranking against your CV, saving jobs, tracking what you applied to, email alerts and event reminders need one. It keeps:

  • Your email address. If you sign in with Google, also the name and profile picture Google shares; your Google password never reaches this site. If you use an email and password instead, only a hash of the password is kept.
  • Your CV and the reading of it described above, if you add one.
  • What you told us you are looking for: the kinds of work, years of experience and the active jobs, internship, graduate and part-time choices, if you save them.
  • For each job you mark as applied or save: its title, employer, link and the date.
  • When you last searched, so a search can mark what is new since your last visit.
  • Which email alerts you turned on, if any, and when the last one was sent.
  • For each careers event you asked to be reminded of: the event, how often, and when the last reminder went.

No tracking, no advertising, no analytics tied to you, and nothing shared with employers or anyone else.

Why we may keep it

  • To give you what you asked for. Your account, CV, preferences, saved and applied jobs are kept because you asked for features that need them (providing the service you signed up for).
  • Because you said yes. Email alerts and event reminders are sent only with your consent, which you give by turning them on and take back by turning them off or using the link in any email.
  • To keep the site working and safe. Our hosts keep short technical logs of requests, including IP addresses, to run the site and stop abuse (a legitimate interest).

How long it is kept

  • Everything in your account stays until you remove it, or delete the account.
  • An event reminder is deleted by itself once the event has happened.
  • Delete your account at the foot of your profile removes all of it at once: the sign-in, the CV file and its reading, every list, alert and reminder. Nothing is archived. If you cannot sign in, email us and we will do it for you.
  • The hosts' technical logs are kept for a short time under their own policies.

Who helps run it

These services process data for us, under contracts that oblige them to protect it:

  • Supabase: accounts, everything an account keeps, and CV files, stored in the EU.
  • Vercel: hosts the website, so it handles each page request, including your IP address.
  • GitHub: runs the jobs and events crawl, and the step that sends alerts and reminders.
  • Google: sends the emails (from {{ contact_email }}, through Gmail), and signs you in if you choose Google.

Vercel, GitHub and Google are based in the United States, so some of this may be handled outside the EU, under the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU and US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies and what your browser loads

One cookie, set only when you sign in, keeps you signed in; it is needed for the site to work, so it needs no banner. Light or dark mode is remembered on your device and never sent anywhere. Fonts, company logos and event pictures are served from this site, so your browser does not contact Google or the event sites to show them. If you signed in with Google, your profile picture loads from Google.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete what is kept about you, object to how it is used, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of it you can do yourself on your profile; for the rest, email {{ contact_email }}. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.

The job and event records themselves

Job adverts are public business information, gathered from companies' own careers pages, and every one links back to the company's original posting, which is where you apply. Careers events are gathered from the sites that list them, and registration is always on their site. This site never takes an application or a booking itself. Employers and organisers can ask for a listing to come down at {{ contact_email }}.
